# Orders are pre-paid; deadline is the Thursday before the consumption week

A Week Plan covers a Monday–Friday consumption week, but ordering and collection happen the
week before: `order_deadline` is the **Thursday preceding `week_start`**. A future reader will
assume "collect every Thursday" means the same week — this records that it is the prior Thursday
(matching how the group operates: money is gathered on Thu for "อาทิตย์หน้า"). Trade-off chosen
over same-week billing so the cook/admin know headcount and have funds before the week starts.
